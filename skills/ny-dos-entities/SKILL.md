name: ny-dos-entities
description: Search New York Department of State entity records and distinguish filed identity or process-address facts from ownership conclusions.
---

Source: https://apps.dos.ny.gov/publicInquiry/ . Use the visible public inquiry form. Inputs: exact organization name or verified DOS ID, entity category, status filter and matching mode. Record all filters and UTC observation time. Registered agent or service-of-process address does not establish beneficial ownership, tenancy or personal residence.

Preserve public result/detail records, DOS ID, entity type, status/as-of date and filing history when available. Keep namesakes separate until identifiers and dated records agree. Limit to three verified aliases and five material filings per session; stop on gates, rate limits or portal errors. Do not order paid records, create an account or contact the agency without explicit authorization.

Schema: query, filters, submitted, source URL, observed_at, outcome, candidate IDs, identity evidence, original-document hashes, uncertainty. No keys or private account records are needed for the public manual path. No automated MCP contract is claimed.

Validation: on 2026-10-19 the OUR UMMAH / BeginsWith / Corporation / AllStatuses submission returned the portal's technical-issue page. This proves attempted submission and failure, not entity absence. Resume from the exact filters when the portal recovers; do not fabricate status or a name-change chain.
