# Investigation method, evidence and claims policy
**Question:** Are Umma Foundation's public representations about organization identity, fundraising, expenditures, operations, partners and advertising supported by reliable evidence? The result may be legitimate, mixed, unverified, or materially misleading; there is no required verdict.

## Investigative hypotheses
H0: Operation substantially matches its representations; odd copy, outsourced marketing, shared office, or regional ad targeting can have benign explanations.
H1: Public content is materially inaccurate or unsupported (impact claims, named projects, logistics, partners).
H2: Filing and expense disclosures differ materially from promotional representations (potentially explainable by accounting bases or reporting periods).
H3: Fundraising marketing misleadingly implies geographic, beneficiary or earmarking details that cannot be substantiated.
H4: Distinct public records establish shared management, payments, contractors, address or operating ties that warrant further verification.

No link to a religious organization or person may be inferred from proximity or presumed religion. The name "Hashem" needs contextual checking; at least one published page specifically identifies a teacher by that name.

## Evidence capture
For every claim store: `claim_id`, `case_id`, normalized entities, source URL/issuer, crawl timestamp + source publication/filing date, underlying file hash, capture location, exact excerpt/page coordinates, parser version, asserted fact, contradictory support, degree of verification, limitation, confidence, and reviewer status. Keep immutable originals separate from derivative notes. Archive public pages lawfully, respecting robots and platform terms.

## Finance methods
Build year-over-year filing panel of program services, fundraising expenses, management, grants, related-party Schedule L, affiliates Schedule R, foreign activities Schedule F, fundraising Schedule G, grant Schedule I, program activity narratives, governance and conflicts. Observe nonpublic revenue/payment processor details cannot be derived from public Form 990 alone; do not fabricate transaction-level cash flows. Normalize tax years vs filing years, amended submissions, restriction of funds, in-kind gifts and restricted reserves. Scrutinize mathematically inconsistent statements and obtain independent corroboration.

## Ad and social methods
Separate the report of an ad impression through an Israeli exit IP from proof of targeting criteria; compare plausible network/platform explanations. Obtain link/video/ad ID and date if available, identify AI/synthetic media carefully and record methodology. Compare narrative against indexed campaigns, marketing agencies, sponsored posts, ad library archives and donation flow. For Reddit, capture competing positions, exact timing, and publicly observable coordination indicators without labeling defenders as bots absent evidence.

## Geographic matching
A one-mile **directory inventory**, not religious/ethnic mapping. Geocode the exact office location and 1,609.344-m radius; gather OSM/Google/HasData/NYC business records with provider attribution, spatial joins, dedup and coverage estimates. Only attach officers/ownership where lawful PUBLIC corporation records directly identify them. Separate registered agent, office tenant, landlord, business contact and beneficial owner. Record source gaps. Render all entities, but emphasize only direct evidence-based ties to case, not unconnected neighbors.

## Publication
Promote claims only when supported by primary records or multiple credible independent sources; avoid defamation, selective evidence and innocent-by-association errors. Maintain an adverse/favorable evidence ledger, corrections and right-of-response workflow. Mark failures to verify as `unknown`, not `false`; do not assert wrongdoing without competent evidence and legal review.
